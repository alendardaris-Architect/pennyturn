Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Your data stays on your device

PennyTurn runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter — inventory items, prices, settings — is saved using your browser's local storage and never sent to our servers. We don't have accounts, and we don't collect or store your personal data.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at google.com/settings/ads or adssettings.google.com.

If you're in the European Economic Area or UK, you'll be asked for consent before any personalized ads are shown, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

Cookies

Aside from cookies set by our advertising partners, this site does not use cookies to track you. Your app data (inventory, preferences) is stored via browser local storage, not cookies, and is only accessible to you on your own device.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views, general region) to improve the site. This data is not tied to your identity.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
